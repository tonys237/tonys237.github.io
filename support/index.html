Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the game, tap the cog for Settings and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought with. Your purchases come back, but their coin bonuses are not given a second time.

How do I change my ad privacy settings?

Tracking: in iOS go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and switch Last Words off (or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"). The game works the same either way.

Personalised ads (UK and EEA): in the game, open Settings > AD PRIVACY and tap CHANGE. Choose Accept or No thanks; with No thanks you only get non-personalised ads.

The Remove Ads purchase stops the ads that play between runs. Videos you choose to watch for a reward are still offered.

More detail is in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone, so deleting the game deletes it. Note this cannot be undone: there is no cloud save. Scores and achievements in Game Center are managed by Apple. For data our ad partners hold, see the privacy policy, or email us and we will help.

The game won't load or keeps closing. What can I do?

  1. Close the game fully (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again.
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Check for an update to the game in the App Store, and to iOS in Settings.
  4. Make sure your iPhone has some free storage.

Please do not delete and reinstall the game to fix it unless you are happy to lose your progress, as progress is stored only on your iPhone. If it still will not load, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.
