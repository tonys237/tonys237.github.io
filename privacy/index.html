Privacy Policy

Last Words: Moto Crash, for iPhone.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who we are

Last Words: Moto Crash ("the game") is made by Tony Soman, trading as Kot Collective, in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email kotcollective@praanaapp.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask you to create an account with us. If you use Game Center, that's your Apple account, handled by Apple. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell your data.
  • Your progress is saved on your iPhone, with two exceptions: a record of your purchases is kept in your iPhone's keychain (it is used to restore purchases and can survive deleting the game), and if you use Game Center, your scores and achievements are held by Apple.
  • The game shows ads from two partners, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, through Unity LevelPlay. They collect some device data to show and measure ads. You choose whether they may track you, and in the UK and EEA you choose whether they may personalise ads.
  • The game sends gameplay and device data to GameAnalytics so we can see how the game is played and improve it. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > ANALYTICS.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Scores and achievements use Apple's Game Center.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your progress on your device: coins, unlocked and equipped items, best distance, the Last Words and Moments you have found, daily missions, the return calendar, your settings and your ad choices. None of this is sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data, except the purchase record kept in your iPhone's keychain, which lets you restore purchases.

The purchase record: to make sure a purchase is never given to you twice, the game keeps a record of the purchases it has already granted in your iPhone's keychain. It stores the purchase transaction IDs only. iOS can keep keychain items after an app is deleted.

When the game starts, it downloads a small settings file from our website, which is hosted by GitHub. Like any website, GitHub sees your device's IP address when it sends the file; the game sends nothing else with that request, and we do not receive or keep that address.

Ads

The game is free and is paid for by ads. You can choose to watch a video for a reward (for example, to keep riding after a crash), and some runs end with a full-screen ad. Ads are provided by:

  • Unity LevelPlay (mediation) and ironSource Ads, both run by ironSource Mobile Ltd., part of Unity Technologies: ironSource privacy policy
  • Unity Ads, run by Unity Technologies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, these partners collect from your device:

  • device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (below), the identifier for vendors (IDFV), and an installation ID of their own;
  • your approximate location (country or region), worked out from your IP address;
  • device and connection details, such as device model, iOS version, language, screen size and connection type;
  • how you interact with the ads (views, taps, video completion), and technical performance data about loading and showing ads;
  • whether you have made purchases in the game (used by Unity Ads).

The partners use this data under their own privacy policies, linked above, and act as independent controllers of it. They may process it outside the United Kingdom.

Tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency)

After your first run, iOS asks whether the game may track you. If you tap Allow, the ad partners can read your advertising identifier to show you ads that are more relevant. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, they cannot read it, and the game works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Ad consent in the UK and EEA

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein or Norway, the game asks you first, before any ad is loaded, whether our ad partners may use data from your device to show you more relevant ads. We also ask players in Switzerland, and anyone whose iPhone's region or time zone points to Europe or whose location we can't tell. If you choose Accept, ads can be personalised. If you choose No thanks, you get non-personalised ads only. Your answer is saved on your device and passed to our ad partners. You can change it at any time in the game: Settings > AD PRIVACY.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads) are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game is told only which product you bought, so it can unlock it. Restore Purchases in Settings asks Apple for your past purchases. See Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses it to post your scores to leaderboards, record achievements and, if you allow it, show your Game Center friends' best runs on the road. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The game keeps a copy of your friends' names and best distances on your device so they show when you are offline.

Crash clips and sharing

The game can make a short video or picture of your crash. It is made on your iPhone and goes nowhere unless you choose to share it, using the iOS share sheet, with whoever you pick. If you choose to save it to Photos, iOS asks permission to add to your library; the game cannot see your photos.

Analytics

To see how the game is played and improve it, the game uses GameAnalytics, which processes the data on our behalf. It receives gameplay events (for example a run starting and ending, the distance reached, what you crashed into, revives, tutorial progress, the Moments and Last Words you find, which ads were shown, and purchases) and device data (device model, iOS version, language, country and IP address). It identifies your device by Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV), which is the same only across our own apps. If you have allowed tracking (see Tracking above), the GameAnalytics software also adds Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). It never receives your name, email or anything you type. You can turn analytics off at any time in the game: Settings > ANALYTICS. See GameAnalytics' privacy and security information.

The game has no crash-reporting service of its own. The ad partners measure ad views and taps, as described under Ads. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports through App Store Connect; that is controlled by Apple in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you are worried about data the ad partners may hold, contact us and we will help you reach them.

Legal basis (UK/EEA)

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, the legal bases for using your data are:

  • Personalised ads: your consent. We ask on the consent screen before any ad is loaded. You can withdraw it at any time in the game: Settings > AD PRIVACY.
  • Analytics (GameAnalytics): legitimate interests. Our interest is seeing how the game is played so we can improve it. You can object at any time by turning it off in Settings > ANALYTICS.
  • Showing non-personalised ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing ad fraud: legitimate interests. Our interest is keeping the game free to play, which the ads pay for.
  • In-app purchases: performance of a contract. When you buy something, the purchase is made with Apple and we use the confirmation to give you what you bought.

International transfers

Our ad partners, ironSource and Unity, may process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. They protect it under their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy arrangements. See the ironSource privacy policy and the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

GameAnalytics processes the analytics data in the United States (on Amazon Web Services), under the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK international data transfer addendum. See GameAnalytics' privacy and security information.

How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you delete the game. The one exception is the purchase record in the keychain, which iOS may keep after the game is deleted; it is used when you restore purchases so nothing is granted twice.
  • Analytics (GameAnalytics): event data for up to 1 year, and a summary used to work out how many players come back for up to 18 months.
  • Ad partners: for as long as their own privacy policies say (linked above).

Your rights and deleting your data

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • restrict how it is used;
  • object to how it is used, including to any use based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability (receive it in a usable format, or have it passed to someone else);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything done with it before you withdrew;
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, in the EEA, your local data protection authority.

How to use them:

  • Data on your iPhone: delete the game.
  • Ad data: turn tracking off (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking), change your choice in Settings > AD PRIVACY in the game, and contact the partners through their privacy policies above to ask for access or deletion.
  • Analytics data: turn it off in Settings > ANALYTICS. To ask about the data GameAnalytics holds for us, email us.
  • Game Center: managed by Apple in iOS Settings and through Apple.
  • Anything else: email kotcollective@praanaapp.com. Apart from the analytics data GameAnalytics holds for us, we hold no personal data about you except any emails you send us, which we keep only as long as needed to answer you.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it does with data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version of the game is released, and mention significant changes in the game's App Store update notes.
